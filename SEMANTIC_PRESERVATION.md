# Semantic preservation checklist

This file guards the paper against conceptual regression during broad editorial passes. It is not a claim that every historical sentence must survive. It identifies ideas whose removal or material weakening requires an explicit decision recorded in the commit message or review notes.

## Required conceptual spine

- Conceptual decomposition asks what a problem or capability is made of, not only which tasks should be performed.
- Changing the conceptual carve changes which dimensions and candidate mechanisms can be represented and routed, potentially making useful solution families visible or generable that an inherited frame would not expose. This is an empirical hypothesis, not a guarantee of novelty or quality.
- Mandatory abstraction locates a concrete problem upward through adjacent genus-and-differentia specializations to `RootSolver`, then retraces the route before decomposing downward.
- The four downward tests are necessity, independence, universality, and completeness; they combine Aristotelian category search with a MECE-like partition discipline without claiming a unique ontology.
- A Solver encapsulates a model, human, program, team, or subtree behind a uniform boundary. A leaf and a composite can present the same outward contract.
- A parent defines how its active children synthesize one higher-level capability. Children may recur under several parents in different edge-local roles.
- The architecture uses emergence in a weak operational sense: the contract may be designed while routes, useful configurations, highways, and depth remain unplaced. Planning artifacts do not demonstrate operational emergence.
- Typed boundaries isolate competing cognitive modes. A rejection may perform structural coaching and trigger ontological accommodation rather than merely request another sample.
- A boundary is an intelligence contributor only if matched experiments show that its separation and exchange change the reachable result.
- Protocol transfer and weight transfer are distinct. Content addressing establishes specification identity, not shared interpretation, execution quality, or cross-domain success.
- Evaluated structures persist; recurrent and validated structures may become reasoning highways. Reuse does not by itself prove improvement through use.
- The Cognitive Commons includes the distribution vision of world-specialized models exposed as reusable thinking protocols, subject to governance, permission, and legitimacy constraints.

## Evidence that must remain correctly bounded

- The mandatory-abstraction run is the canonical construction artifact. It demonstrates structurally checked, model-authored upward routes, higher-parent insertion, multi-parent reuse, and declared nested composition. It does not establish ontology correctness, independent child execution, learning, emergence, or outcome gains.
- The original Gemini planning artifact is superseded and retained only for reproducibility and error provenance. Its unintended 29-node startup scaffold made reuse seed-dominated; it demonstrates routing and mutation through supplied structure, not discovery, gate behavior, or compounding.
- The Ontology companion study provides positive descriptive evidence of persistent, revisable, typed population state and recorded redirection chains. Its unmatched conditions do not isolate improved creativity, framing, solution quality, or search efficiency.
- Strong claims deliberately withdrawn from earlier versions must not silently return: negligible amortized overhead, contract sufficiency, automatic improvement through reuse, canonical decompositions, depth as accuracy, or causal superiority over a matched monolithic baseline.
